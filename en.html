FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is the app coming out?

Soon, on the App Store. There’s no exact date yet — leave your email and we’ll write to you once, when the app is live.

Will there be an Android version?

For now it’s iPhone only. Leave your email and we’ll let you know about the launch.

Where is my data stored?

In your own cloud account, fully synced. New iPhone or a reinstall — just sign in and everything is there. Only you can see your data. Sign in with email, Apple or Google.

Which languages does the app support?

Ukrainian, English and Russian.

I have 2–3 students. Do I have to pay?

No. The free version covers up to 3 students, 1 lesson-pack template, the schedule with one-tap check-off and the lesson journal — with no time limit.

How do I cancel Pro?

Anytime, in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Pro is $6.99 a month or $24.99 a year (the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial); the App Store shows the price in your currency.

Which iPhone do I need?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How can I contact you?

Email us at supportschedulelesson@gmail.com — we’ll get back to you.
